Moss’s Club house. Write a worry.
Moss delivers it.

Someone in another city reads it and writes back.

Coming soon to iPhone and Android

How it works

  1. 01

    Write

    One letter a day, on a typewriter. No name, no profile.

  2. 02

    Moss delivers

    Your letter goes to another city. You get up to 3 letters a day, too.

  3. 03

    Get a reply

    Someone picks your letter and writes back. You can send a thank-you.

Good to know

  • People write every letter. AI only checks and translates them.
  • Others see your letter and an approximate city. Nothing else.
  • Free. Optional rewarded ads are available in the shop for ages 19 and up.

Report or block a letter right from the letter. If you might be in danger, don’t wait for a reply. Contact local emergency services now.
