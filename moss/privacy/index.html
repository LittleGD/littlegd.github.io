Moss’s Club house Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 4, 2026

Moss’s Club house (the “app”) is made by Jongmin Lee, an independent developer at JML.Studio (“we”). This policy explains what information the app uses, why, who helps us process it and how long we keep it.

Summary

  • Other users never see your name or email. They see your letter and an approximate city.
  • Letters and replies are sent to OpenAI for safety review and translation. OpenAI does not write them.
  • Your approximate city comes from your network connection. The app doesn’t use GPS.
  • No third-party ads or behavioral tracking. We count links opened to our own apps by day, without analytics or advertising SDKs.
  • You can delete your account and letters in the app at any time.

1. Information we collect

Account. When you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive an account identifier and your email address (Apple may give us a private relay address). We don’t store your name or profile photo from these providers. If you use Sign in with Apple, we also keep an encrypted Apple token so we can revoke your Apple sign-in when you delete your account.

Profile. Your app language, your time zone, your approximate city, the version of the terms you accepted and when you were last active.

Letters and replies. The text of letters and replies you write, their translations, review results, thank-you notes, and delivery details such as dates and which letters you were offered or chose.

Safety records. Reports you send, reports about your content, a copy of the reported content as the reporter saw it, the people you block, and account restrictions.

Notifications. If you allow notifications, your device’s push token and platform.

Promotion taps. When you open a link to one of our other apps, the app sends the app, link placement, platform and destination. The request uses your signed-in session. The daily aggregate table stores counts by those fields without account IDs or IP addresses.

Messages to us. If you email us, your email address and what you write.

Feedback. In-app feedback includes its type and text, language, platform, app version, receipt time and linked account.

Local drafts. Unsent drafts are stored on your device and are not submitted to the server until you send them. Android backup rules exclude app data from automatic cloud backup and device transfer. On iOS, the folder holding drafts is marked as excluded from backup. This setting guides the OS and does not guarantee that backups or restores are prevented. Burning a draft does not erase copies in past backups.

2. Approximate location

Moss needs to know roughly where letters travel from and to. Our hosting provider estimates a city from your IP address. We round coordinates to about 10 km and store the approximate city separately from security records. Readers see only that city. The app does not request or use device location services.

For sign-in security, temporary rate-limit records contain IP-based keys. A scheduled cleanup removes records more than two days after their last request; delays in that job can delay removal. Session records do not store IP addresses or User-Agent values. This does not mean our hosting provider keeps no network or security logs.

3. How we use information

We use this information to run the app: to sign you in, deliver letters between cities and time zones, translate letters, review content for safety, show help resources when a letter suggests someone may need them, send notifications, handle reports and blocks, prevent abuse and rate limit requests, fix problems and answer your messages. We also use aggregate link counts to measure promotion of our own apps. We do not sell your information, build personal advertising profiles or target ads to you.

4. AI review and translation

Before a letter or reply is delivered, we send its text, its language and, for replies, the original letter as context, to OpenAI. OpenAI checks it against our letter rules and translates it. AI never writes or edits your letters. Before each letter, reply or revision is sent, the app asks for your agreement to this OpenAI processing, separately from acceptance of the Terms of Use. It sends the content only after you agree.

We send requests with OpenAI’s storage option turned off. According to OpenAI’s API policies, API data is not used to train their models by default, but OpenAI may keep it for a limited period to monitor abuse. See OpenAI’s Privacy Policy.

5. Service providers

We use a small number of providers to run the app:

  • Cloudflare hosts our server and database and estimates your city from your IP address.
  • OpenAI reviews and translates letters and replies.
  • Apple and Google provide sign-in, and deliver notifications through their push services.
  • Expo relays notifications to Apple and Google. Notifications contain no letter text.
  • GitHub processes in-app feedback in a private repository, not public issues.

These providers may process information in the United States and other countries. They handle it under their own terms and privacy policies.

6. How long we keep information

We keep your account and letters while your account exists. A letter travels for at most 14 days. If nobody replies, it comes back to you. You can burn a returned letter and its reply, which deletes their text, translations and review data and leaves only a minimal record that the letter existed.

When you delete your account, we delete the account, sessions, your letters and replies you wrote. We revoke the Apple sign-in tokens available to us and guide you if you need to remove Apple access manually. We remove linked private GitHub feedback asynchronously within 7 days; deletion is complete only after that cleanup. To review safety issues, we retain reports you filed or received, including their reason, reported content and handling status, without user IDs for up to 90 days, then delete them. The text may still identify someone. Cloudflare D1 recovery history may retain deleted data for up to 30 days, depending on the plan. Other provider records follow their own retention rules.

After delivery, we clear feedback text from our Cloudflare D1 database. We delete the raw feedback issue and related copies no later than 90 days after it is closed or 180 days after receipt, whichever comes first. Unresolved feedback is deleted within 180 days of receipt.

After a deletion request is completed, we keep verification evidence and request metadata until the earlier of 90 days after completion or 180 days after receipt. For unfinished requests, we retain the minimum needed to complete the work.

7. Your choices and rights

You can delete your account in Settings > Delete account. You can turn notifications off in your device settings. You can block anyone who writes to you.

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your information, or to object to or restrict how we use it. To make a request, email jmjplearner@gmail.com. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

8. Children

The app is not intended for children under 13. If you believe a child under that age is using the app, contact us and we will delete the account.

9. Security

Data is sent over HTTPS, access to our systems is limited, and our logs record error codes, not letter text or account IDs. No service can be perfectly secure, so please don’t put identifying information in your letters.

10. This website

This website, including the page where this policy is published, uses Google Analytics 4 to understand visits. Google may receive browser and device information, an approximate location from your IP address, the pages you view and cookies or similar identifiers, under Google’s Privacy Policy. The app itself does not use Google Analytics.

11. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above. If the change matters, we will tell you in the app before it takes effect.
