Moss’s Club house Support

Questions, or something not working? Write to us.

Contact

jmjplearner@gmail.com

We review and handle in-app feedback once a week. Tell us your phone model, its OS version and what happened on which screen, and we can help faster. You can write in any language. Please don’t include the text of anyone else’s letter. Safety reports are handled separately.

Delete your account

In the app, open Settings and tap Delete account. You can also request deletion using the link below or by emailing us. You do not need to reinstall the app.

Request account deletion

For a request outside the app, we verify you using a single-use confirmation link sent to the email address registered with your account, including an Apple private relay address if you use one. Where available, you may instead reauthenticate with the same sign-in provider. We do not ask for an ID, password or letter text.

We aim to acknowledge requests within 7 days and delete the account within 7 days of verification, usually within 28 days in total. Linked private feedback is removed asynchronously. We do not consider deletion complete until that cleanup is done. See the Privacy Policy for records kept for safety.

Reporting a letter

To report a letter or reply, open it in the app and tap Report letter. Your reason and the content as you saw it reach our operators privately, so you don’t need to copy anything. You can also tap Block this sender so you won’t exchange letters with that person again.

If you are in danger

Moss’s Club house can’t respond to emergencies. If you or someone else might be in immediate danger, contact local emergency services or someone nearby now. Don’t wait for a letter reply.

  • Do I need an account?

    Yes. Sign in with Apple or Google so your letters can find their way back to you. Other people never see your name or email.

  • How many letters can I write?

    One worry letter a day. Each day Moss also brings you up to three letters from other people. Choose one to answer, and you have 48 hours to write your reply.

  • My letter came back without a reply

    Sometimes nobody picks a letter in time. When that happens, Moss brings it back to you. You can write a new letter the next day.

  • Who reads my letter?

    The person who receives it. Before delivery, an automated review checks the letter and translates it into the reader’s language. AI does not write or change what you say. If a letter needs changes before it can be sent, the app tells you.

  • Can people see where I live?

    No. Moss only uses an approximate city based on your network connection, and readers see only that approximate city. The app doesn’t use your phone’s GPS.

  • What does burning a letter do?

    Holding to burn a draft clears it from your phone without sending it. After a reply comes back, you can also burn the letter and its reply to remove them from your archive. This can’t be undone.

  • Does it cost anything?

    No. The app and letter exchange are free, and there are no purchases. After opening the free set of three boxes, you can claim one spark a day if you are 18 or younger, or choose to watch a rewarded ad if you are 19 or older. On iOS, ad personalization follows your tracking permission and regional consent. On Android, personalized ads are off by default and can be enabled in Settings. When personalization is not permitted, general ads may be requested. The choice does not change the reward odds or daily limit.
