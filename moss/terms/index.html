Moss’s Club house Terms of Use

Effective date: November 9, 2026

Moss’s Club house (the “app”) is made by Jongmin Lee, an independent developer at JML.Studio (“we”). By creating an account or using the app, you agree to these terms. If you don’t agree, please don’t use the app.

1. What the app is

The app lets people exchange anonymous letters about their worries. You can write one letter a day. Other people may receive it and write one reply. Letters are reviewed automatically and translated so they can travel between languages.

The app is not therapy, medical, legal or crisis support. Replies come from other users, not from professionals, and we don’t check whether their advice is right for you. If you might be in danger, contact local emergency services right away.

2. Who can use it

You must be at least 14 years old to use the app. If you are under the age of majority where you live, you need permission from a parent or guardian. You may not use the app if we have restricted your account or if the law where you live forbids it.

3. Your account

You sign in with Apple or Google. Keep your device and sign-in secure, because you are responsible for what is sent from your account. One person, one account: don’t create accounts to get around a restriction.

4. Letter rules

Moss’s Club house works because people are kind to strangers. When you write a letter or a reply, you agree not to:

  • harass, threaten, insult or shame anyone, or encourage anyone to hurt themselves or others;
  • post hate speech or content that attacks people for who they are;
  • post sexual content, or any content that sexualizes minors;
  • share personal information that could identify you or anyone else, such as names, phone numbers, addresses, social media accounts or photos;
  • ask to meet, move the conversation elsewhere, or ask for money;
  • advertise, spam, or send links;
  • post content that is illegal, or that infringes someone else’s rights;
  • pretend to be someone else, or use automated means to send letters.

We have zero tolerance for objectionable content and abusive users. You can talk about hard feelings and painful experiences. That is what the app is for. What matters is how you treat the people reading.

5. Review, reporting and blocking

Every letter and reply is checked before delivery by an automated review that uses AI. A letter may be delivered, returned to you to revise, held, or not delivered. The review can make mistakes, and it doesn’t guarantee that every letter you receive will be appropriate.

You can report any letter or reply you receive, and you can block its sender. We review reports and act on objectionable content as quickly as we can, aiming for within 24 hours. We may remove content, and we may restrict or end accounts that break these terms, with or without notice. A restricted account can’t send, reply or receive new letters.

You give us a worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free license to store, review, translate, deliver and display your letters and replies to operate the app, and to keep them as described in the Privacy Policy. Once a letter is delivered, its reader can read it, and we can’t control what a reader does with what they read.

Don’t send anything you aren’t comfortable sharing with a stranger.

7. Free service and changes

After opening your free set of three shop boxes, you can earn one daily reward. If you are 18 or younger, you can claim one spark for checking in, without an ad. If you are 19 or older, you can choose to finish a rewarded ad when available. On iOS, ad personalization follows the tracking permission and regional consent. On Android, personalized ads are off by default and can be enabled in Settings. When personalization is not permitted, general ads may be requested. Both have the same reward odds and daily limit. The chances are 1 spark: 2%; 2: 20%; 3: 50%; 4: 20%; 5: 8%. Both methods share one reward limit per account per day, resetting at local midnight. You can correct your age group in Settings. You can use sparks to open shop boxes. Sparks and items have no cash value and cannot be purchased with real money, transferred or redeemed for cash.
