Tell us your device, its iOS version and what happened on which screen, and we can help faster.

  • Do I need to sign in?

    No, you can start right away without an account. Sign in with Apple or Google only if you want to continue on another device.

  • I want to continue on another device

    Sign in with the same account on both devices. Your progress stays in sync on its own.

  • I want to delete my account

    Go to My voyage > Settings > Data & account and tap Delete account. Your account and the progress on our server are deleted right away, and this can't be undone. To keep your account and start over, tap Reset all learning data in the same place.

  • Where do my recordings go?

    Shadowing recordings stay on your device and are deleted when you close the shadowing screen. Speech recognition runs on the device too.

  • Which devices does it run on?

    iPhone and iPad with iOS 18 or later.
